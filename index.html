1. Situation

Fly either side from a 3D cockpit. Fuel burns, engines spool, drop tanks feed first, heavy stores cost turn rate, and sustained G greys you out and can knock you out. Radars scan bar by bar and lose beaming targets in the notch; ridges block them outright. Semi-active missiles need your lock to impact; heat-seekers see a burner from much further away.
